Project Manager and Chief Inspector of highly demanding infrastructure structures

Tampere

Bridge inspector and concrete structure condition surveyor who has inspected the bridges and tunnels of the Helsinki Metro for eight years. His background combines studies in materials and rock engineering, a degree in infrastructure engineering and work as a laboratory manager in concrete condition surveys. He is at his best in inspections where conditions are out of the ordinary: at sea, in the metro, on bridges and at dams.
